🛂 Step 1 for everyone: you must enter mainland China legally

Hong Kong and Macao are separate immigration areas from mainland China. Crossing into Zhuhai means a real border crossing — passport (plus visa if required), or the relevant permit for HK/Macao/Taiwan residents. See the Visa & Entry section.

🚢Ferry: China Ferry Terminal / HK–Macau Ferry Terminal → Jiuzhou PortRECOMMENDED
  • Two departure terminals, both to Zhuhai Jiuzhou Port: China Ferry Terminal (Tsim Sha Tsui, Kowloon) and the Hong Kong–Macau Ferry Terminal (Sheung Wan, Hong Kong Island).
  • China Ferry Terminal → Jiuzhou Port: about 70 minutes, roughly HK$170–200 ordinary class; around 6–7 sailings a day (approx. 07:35 / 09:30 / 11:30 / 13:30 / 15:30 / 17:45 / 20:30 — check the day's timetable).
  • HK–Macau Ferry Terminal (Sheung Wan) → Jiuzhou Port: about 70 minutes, roughly HK$200 ordinary class or HK$300 deluxe; around 7–8 sailings a day (approx. 08:30 / 10:30 / 12:30 / 14:30 / 16:30 / 19:30 / 21:30).
  • Operator: TurboJET (turbojet.com.hk); tickets are also sold on Trip.com and similar platforms. Some sailings are indirect and call at the other terminal — usually slightly cheaper.
  • Book online in advance. Saturday 14 November is a busy travel day and morning / early-afternoon sailings fill up.
  • Two immigration checks: exit Hong Kong at the ferry terminal, then clear mainland China entry at Jiuzhou Port (this is where international guests complete their entry formalities). Allow 1–1.5 hours.
  • Jiuzhou Port → Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel: taxi or DiDi, about 35–45 minutes. Free shuttle buses run from the port towards Gongbei and Xiangzhou.
  • A HKIA ↔ Jiuzhou Port ferry (~60 min) also exists, but it is only for passengers holding an onward flight ticket — not for guests being met on arrival.
🚗Drive across the HZMB: chauffeur car or self-drive (“Northbound Travel”)RECOMMENDED

Two ways: a cross-border chauffeur car (easiest) or self-driving under the Northbound Travel scheme if you already keep a car in Hong Kong.

① Cross-border chauffeur car / 7-seat MPV (best with luggage or elderly guests)

  • Hong Kong (including HKG airport) → Hengqin / Chimelong: about 90 minutes via the HZMB, from roughly HK$1,200–1,900 one way (Alphard 7-seat and similar).
  • Fares are usually all-inclusive — driver, fuel, bridge and tunnel tolls. You are dropped at the hotel entrance; no luggage handling.
  • Give the driver “Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel” as the drop-off, and book 2–3 days ahead — weekend cars fill up.
  • Note: on the HZMB, passengers do get out at both HK Port and the Zhuhai Highway Port to clear immigration (about 5–10 minutes each). This is different from the stay-in-car Shenzhen Bay route.

② Self-drive: “Northbound Travel for Hong Kong Vehicles”

  • HK private cars may enter and leave the mainland only through the HZMB Zhuhai Highway Port and may drive within Guangdong Province. The scheme now runs until 1 June 2031.
  • Requirements: the applicant must be the registered owner and hold a Hong Kong identity card plus a Home Return Permit; every nominated driver must hold both a valid full HK driving licence and a mainland driving licence; the car must be a non-commercial private car with 8 seats or fewer and under 6 m long.
  • Process: register and enter the ballot at hzmbqfs.gov.hk for a quota → vehicle inspection (about HK$400–600) → insurance (mainland compulsory motor insurance or an “equivalent recognition” HK policy, roughly ¥1,000–3,000 a year) → complete the filing.
  • Limits: no more than 30 consecutive days per entry, no more than 180 days per calendar year; driving is limited to Guangdong Province.
  • Bridge toll: ¥150 per trip for small passenger cars (private cars and taxis). Mainland-standard ETC (YueTong card) gets a 5% discount. Manual lanes accept RMB cash, bank cards, Alipay and WeChat Pay.
  • Route: HK Port → the 42 km main bridge (no petrol stations, no toilets, no U-turns) → Zhuhai Highway Port → about 40 minutes to the hotel.
  • Passenger clearance at the Zhuhai Highway Port runs 08:00–22:00; overnight traffic uses a separate lane arrangement. The HK Port has a local car park (~673 private car spaces; book ahead at hzmbparking.com.hk) if you prefer to park and take the shuttle.
🚍 Backup: HZMB shuttle bus (“Golden Bus”)
  • Route: HZMB Hong Kong Port → HZMB Zhuhai Port. Around 30–40 minutes, runs 24 hours.
  • Fare: HK$65 daytime (06:00–23:59), HK$70 overnight; children and seniors half price.
  • Frequency: every ~5 minutes at peak, every 15–30 minutes late at night.
  • Reaching the HK Port: “A-prefix” airport buses stop there (~HK$30–50), or take the MTR to Tung Chung then bus B6.
  • Tickets: the “HZMB Shuttle Bus” WeChat official account, the official website, or counters / self-service machines at the port.
  • Luggage: one item free per person (max 20 kg, total dimensions under 158 cm).
🚌 Backup: cross-border coach
  • Operators: Trans-Island (CTS), China Travel Service, Eternal East and others.
  • Pick-up points across Kowloon, Hong Kong Island and the New Territories.
  • Drop-off points include Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel, Chimelong Penguin Hotel and other Hengqin hotels.
  • Around HK$150 one way from Kowloon / New Territories; ~HK$230 from HK Airport.
  • Book online in advance — weekend services fill up. Be at the stop 15 minutes early.
🚆 Backup: high-speed rail via Guangzhou South
  • Hong Kong West Kowloon → Guangzhou South → Zhuhai Station (immigration is handled inside West Kowloon Station).
  • From Zhuhai Station take the intercity line to Zhuhai Chimelong Station — the closest stop to the hotel.
  • Zhuhai Station and Hengqin Station both connect onward to Hengqin / Macao.

🛂Visa & EntryImportant

Do this first, before booking flights. Many nationalities can now enter China visa-free for up to 30 days — but it depends on your passport, the purpose of your visit and how long you stay.

Three-step self-check

  1. Passport type — the visa-free arrangements apply to holders of ordinary passports only.
  2. Nationality — check the list below (50 countries as of September 2026).
  3. Purpose & length — business, tourism, visiting family or friends, exchange visits and transit for no more than 30 days are covered. Work, study and journalism require a visa.

🔄 Other ways you may not need a visa
  • Mutual visa exemption (29 countries incl. Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, the UAE, Qatar, Serbia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Belarus): stay up to 30 days per entry.
  • 240-hour (10-day) transit visa-free: citizens of 54 countries holding an onward ticket to a third country or region may enter via 60 designated ports, including in Guangdong, and stay up to 240 hours.
  • 24-hour transit visa-free: open to all nationalities, provided you stay within the port's restricted area.
  • Regional schemes: e.g. Hainan offers 30-day visa-free entry to citizens of 61 countries.

Verify with official sources

  • cs.mfa.gov.cn — Chinese Consular Service, official Q&A on the unilateral visa-free policy.
  • nia.gov.cn — National Immigration Administration, the original policy texts.
  • 12367 immigration hotline (24 hours): +86 20 12367 for Guangdong.
  • visaforchina.cn — Chinese Visa Application Service Centres; pick your country or region to see the document checklist.
📝 If you do need a visa — how to apply (Hong Kong example)
  • Application centre: Chinese Visa Application Service Centre, Unit 304, 3/F, Causeway Center, 28 Harbour Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong. Tel +852 3612 5730 · hongkongcenter@visaforchina.org
  • Process: ① register and submit the form and documents online at visaforchina.cn → ② once the online review passes, bring your original passport and one photo (taken within 6 months, 48 × 33 mm) to the centre within 30 days, on weekdays 9:00–15:00, and give fingerprints → ③ pay and collect.
  • Fingerprints are waived for applicants under 14 or over 70, and — until 31 December 2026 — for all short-stay visas of 180 days or less.
  • Common categories: L (tourism), M (business), Q2 / S2 (visiting relatives), F (exchange visits). Attending a wedding is normally applied for as tourism (L) or visiting friends.
  • Fees: service fee HK$240 for regular processing (express options cost more); visa fees vary by nationality and several have been reduced or waived until 31 December 2026.
  • Timing: apply 3–4 weeks ahead in peak season.
  • If you are applying in your home country instead, use your local Chinese embassy, consulate or visa application centre — requirements are broadly similar.

🪪DocumentsHK / Macao / Taiwan

If you hold a Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan resident status, the rules below apply to you instead of a visa. Always travel with the original document — copies are not accepted at the border.

Hong Kong / Macao residents · Home Return Permit

  • Chinese nationals: 10-year validity (aged 18+) or 5-year (under 18); unlimited stays within validity.
  • Non-Chinese permanent residents: 5-year permit, max 90 days per stay (extendable by up to 90 days).
  • You can renew when less than 6 months remain — check yours now.
  • Apply through CTS (China Travel Service) in Hong Kong by online appointment only; standard processing about 12 working days, express about 5 working days (+HK$200).
  • Renewal can also be done inside the mainland: about 7 working days, roughly ¥350.

Taiwan residents · Taiwan Compatriot Permit

  • A 5-year Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents is required; check both validity and endorsement status.
✅ Registered residents can use the automated / co-inspection e-channels. Since 18 August 2026, travellers crossing at the HZMB, Hengqin and Qingmao ports may use either their resident ID card or Home Return Permit — one document, one passage.

Money, internet, weather

  • Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay both accept international cards — set them up before you travel. Carry a little cash (RMB) as a backup. Tipping is not expected.
  • Internet: arrange roaming or an eSIM before departure; a local SIM is also easy to buy in Zhuhai.
  • Getting around: DiDi is the standard ride-hailing app in China (English interface available); the driver will call you, so having a local number or in-app chat helps.
  • Weather: mid-November in Zhuhai is around 20–26°C by day and cooler in the evening — bring a light jacket.
  • Language: English is limited outside hotels. Save the Chinese names of the hotel and the venue on your phone to show drivers.
🚫 Avoid bringing fresh eggs, live animals or plants, and excessive tobacco or alcohol. Cash over ¥20,000 (or foreign currency equivalent) must be declared.
