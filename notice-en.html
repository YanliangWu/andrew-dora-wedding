🛂 Hong Kong and Macao are separate immigration areas from mainland China — crossing into Zhuhai is a real border entry. Bring your passport (plus visa if required), or the relevant permit for HK / Macao / Taiwan residents. See Visa & Entry.
🚢Ferry: China Ferry Terminal / HK–Macau Ferry Terminal → Jiuzhou PortRECOMMENDED
  • Two departure terminals: China Ferry Terminal (Tsim Sha Tsui) and the HK–Macau Ferry Terminal (Sheung Wan).
  • From Tsim Sha Tsui: about 70 minutes, roughly HK$170–200; about 6–7 sailings a day (07:35 / 09:30 / 11:30 / 13:30 / 15:30 / 17:45 / 20:30).
  • From Sheung Wan: about 70 minutes, roughly HK$200 (deluxe HK$300); about 7–8 sailings a day (08:30 / 10:30 / 12:30 / 14:30 / 16:30 / 19:30 / 21:30).
  • Tickets: TurboJET (turbojet.com.hk) or Trip.com. Saturday is a busy travel day — book ahead.
  • Two immigration checks (exit HK at the terminal, enter China at Jiuzhou Port — this is where international guests complete entry formalities): allow 1–1.5 hours, then a 35–45 min taxi to the hotel.
  • A HKIA ↔ Jiuzhou Port ferry (~60 min) exists but is only for passengers holding an onward flight ticket.
🚗Drive across the HZMB: chauffeur car or self-drive (“Northbound Travel”)RECOMMENDED

A cross-border chauffeur car is easiest; self-driving suits guests who already keep a car in Hong Kong.

① Cross-border chauffeur car / 7-seat MPV

  • Hong Kong (including HKG airport) → Chimelong: about 90 minutes, from roughly HK$1,200–1,900 one way (Alphard 7-seat and similar).
  • Usually all-inclusive (driver, fuel, bridge and tunnel tolls), dropped at the hotel entrance. Give “Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel” as the drop-off and book 2–3 days ahead — weekend cars fill up.
  • On the bridge, passengers get out at both HK Port and the Zhuhai Highway Port to clear immigration (about 5–10 minutes each).

② Self-drive: “Northbound Travel for Hong Kong Vehicles”

  • May enter and leave the mainland only through the HZMB Zhuhai Highway Port, driving limited to Guangdong Province; the scheme runs until 1 June 2031.
  • Register and enter the ballot at hzmbqfs.gov.hk for a quota, then complete inspection, insurance and filing; every nominated driver needs both a full HK licence and a mainland licence.
  • Toll ¥150 per trip (5% off with mainland-standard ETC); no more than 30 consecutive days per entry and 180 days per calendar year.
  • The 42 km main bridge has no petrol stations, no toilets and no U-turns; the Zhuhai Highway Port is open 24 hours (passenger halls 08:00–22:00).
🚍 Other options: Golden Bus / cross-border coach / high-speed rail
  • Golden Bus (HZMB shuttle): Hong Kong Port → Zhuhai Port, 30–40 minutes, runs 24 hours; HK$65 daytime, HK$70 overnight, every ~5 minutes at peak. Tickets: the “HZMB Shuttle Bus” WeChat account, the official site, or machines at the port.
  • Cross-border coach: pick-up points across Kowloon, Hong Kong Island and the New Territories; drop-off at Chimelong Hengqin Bay Hotel; around HK$150 one way. Book ahead and be at the stop 15 minutes early.
  • High-speed rail: Hong Kong West Kowloon → Guangzhou South → Zhuhai Station (immigration handled inside West Kowloon), then the intercity line to Zhuhai Chimelong Station, with a resort shuttle at the exit.

🛂Visa & EntryImportant

Do this first, before booking flights: many nationalities can now enter China visa-free for up to 30 days, depending on your passport, your purpose of visit and how long you stay.

  • Around 50 countries currently qualify for visa-free entry of up to 30 days on an ordinary passport — tourism, visiting family or friends, exchange visits and transit. Work, study and journalism require a visa.
  • Other grounds include mutual visa exemption (Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia and 26 others) and 240-hour transit visa-free.
  • The list and validity periods do change — check an official source before you travel: cs.mfa.gov.cn, nia.gov.cn, or the 12367 immigration hotline +86 20 12367 (24 hours).
📝 If you do need a visa — how to apply (Hong Kong example)
  • Apply at visaforchina.cn — fill in the form and upload documents online. In Hong Kong the centre is at Unit 304, 3/F, Causeway Center, 28 Harbour Road, Wan Chai (+852 3612 5730).
  • Once the online review passes, bring your original passport and one photo (within 6 months, 48 × 33 mm) to the centre within 30 days, weekdays 9:00–15:00, and give fingerprints (waived under 14 or over 70).
  • Attending a wedding is normally applied for as tourism (L) or visiting friends; service fee HK$240 for regular processing. Apply 3–4 weeks ahead in peak season.
✉️ If your application needs an invitation letter, just contact us — we will send a bilingual one. Allow a few weeks.
📌 Two things matter at the border: your purpose of entry must match your visa or visa-free basis, and you must not overstay. Carry your passport at all times — hotels register your stay with the police automatically.

🪪DocumentsHK / Macao / Taiwan

If you hold a Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan resident status, the rules below apply to you instead of a visa. Always travel with the original document — copies are not accepted at the border.

Hong Kong / Macao residents · Home Return Permit

  • Chinese nationals: 10-year validity (aged 18+) or 5-year (under 18). Non-Chinese permanent residents: 5-year permit, max 90 days per stay.
  • You can renew when less than 6 months remain — check yours now.
  • Apply through CTS (China Travel Service) in Hong Kong by online appointment only: about 12 working days standard, 5 working days express; HK$390 adult, HK$260 child.
  • Renewal can also be done inside the mainland: about 7 working days, roughly ¥350.

Taiwan residents · Taiwan Compatriot Permit

  • A 5-year Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents is required; check both validity and endorsement status.
✅ Registered residents can use the automated / co-inspection e-channels. Since 18 August 2026, travellers crossing at the HZMB, Hengqin and Qingmao ports may use either their resident ID card or Home Return Permit — one document, one passage.

🕐 The HZMB port itself is open 24 hours, but the passenger clearance halls close at 22:00 — late-night crossings must go through the accompanied-vehicle channel. If you are arriving by ferry, note that Jiuzhou Port closes at 21:00.

Money, internet, weather

  • Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay both accept international cards — set them up before you travel. Carry a little cash (RMB) as a backup. Tipping is not expected.
  • Internet: arrange roaming or an eSIM before departure; a local SIM is also easy to buy in Zhuhai.
  • Getting around: DiDi is the standard ride-hailing app in China (English interface available); the driver will call you, so having a local number or in-app chat helps.
  • Weather: mid-November in Zhuhai is around 20–26°C by day and cooler in the evening — bring a light jacket.
  • Language: English is limited outside hotels. Save the Chinese names of the hotel and the venue on your phone to show drivers.
🚫 Avoid bringing fresh eggs, live animals or plants, and excessive tobacco or alcohol. Cash over ¥20,000 (or foreign currency equivalent) must be declared.
